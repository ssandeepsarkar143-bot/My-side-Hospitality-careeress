Hospitality Job Categories

Front Office Jobs

Front Office is the face of every hotel — handling guest check-in, check-out, reservations, concierge service and guest relations. These roles require excellent communication, a calm presence and strong problem-solving skills.

Common roles: Front Office Manager, Duty Manager, Guest Service Associate (GSA), Front Office Executive, Reservations Executive, Concierge, Bell Captain, Night Auditor, Telephone Operator.
Apply for Front Office jobs

Food & Beverage Service Jobs

F&B Service covers all guest-facing dining roles — restaurants, bars, banquets, room service and in-room dining. Roles span from entry-level Stewards through Restaurant Managers and Beverage Managers.

Common roles: F&B Manager, Restaurant Manager, Bar Manager, Captain, Bartender, Steward, Banquet Manager, In-Room Dining (IRD) Executive, Sommelier, Barista.
Apply for F&B Service jobs

Kitchen / Culinary / Chef Jobs

Kitchen and culinary roles cover every brigade position — Indian, Continental, Chinese, Bakery, Pastry, Tandoor and Cold Kitchen. Hotels, fine-dining restaurants and cloud kitchens across India hire continuously.

Common roles: Executive Chef, Sous Chef, Chef de Partie (CDP), Demi Chef de Partie (DCDP), Commis I/II/III, Pastry Chef, Baker, Tandoor Chef, Continental Chef, Indian Chef, Kitchen Steward.
Apply for Kitchen / Chef jobs

Housekeeping Jobs

Housekeeping ensures every guest experiences a spotless, well-presented hotel. This department drives guest reviews more than any other and offers strong career growth from Room Attendant to Executive Housekeeper.

Common roles: Executive Housekeeper, Assistant Housekeeper, Floor Supervisor, Housekeeping Supervisor, Room Attendant, Public Area Attendant, Linen Room Attendant, Laundry Manager, Horticulturist.
Apply for Housekeeping jobs

Spa & Wellness Jobs

Resort and city-hotel spas hire certified therapists, beauticians and wellness leaders across Ayurveda, Swedish, Thai and signature therapies. Demand is strong in Goa, Kerala, Rajasthan and metro luxury hotels.

Common roles: Spa Manager, Spa Therapist, Ayurveda Therapist, Beauty Therapist, Salon Manager, Yoga Instructor, Fitness Instructor, Recreation Executive.
Apply for Spa & Wellness jobs

Sales & Marketing Jobs

Hotel sales drives revenue across corporate, MICE, travel-trade and OTA channels. Marketing covers social media, brand, PR and digital. Strong growth path into Director of Sales and Commercial Director roles.

Common roles: Director of Sales, Sales Manager (Corporate / MICE / Travel Trade), Assistant Sales Manager, Sales Executive, Marketing Manager, Digital Marketing Executive, PR Manager, Revenue Manager.
Apply for Sales & Marketing jobs

Hotel Security Jobs

Hotel security teams handle access control, fire safety, CCTV monitoring, VIP movements and emergency response. Ex-defence and ex-police candidates are highly valued in this department.

Common roles: Chief Security Officer, Security Manager, Security Supervisor, Security Officer, Loss Prevention Officer, Fire & Safety Officer.
Apply for Security jobs

Human Resources Jobs

Hotel HR teams manage talent acquisition, payroll, training, employee engagement and statutory compliance for large multi-shift workforces. Industrial-relations exposure is a strong plus for senior roles.

Common roles: Director of HR, HR Manager, Assistant HR Manager, HR Executive, Training Manager, L&D Manager, Talent Acquisition Specialist, Payroll Executive.
Apply for HR jobs
