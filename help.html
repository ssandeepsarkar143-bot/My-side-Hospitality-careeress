How do I create an account on Hospitality Careers?
Visit the homepage and click "Login / Register". Choose "Sign Up" and fill in your details (name, email, password). You can also sign in with your Google account for faster access. Once registered, you'll be directed to your personal dashboard.
What is MPIN and how do I use it?
MPIN is a 4-digit security PIN used to protect your dashboard access. For Owners, you set your own MPIN on first login. For Admins, the Owner sets your MPIN from the Admin Management section. You must enter your MPIN every time you access the dashboard. If you forget your MPIN, contact the system owner.
How do I apply for a job?
Browse jobs from your dashboard's "Find Job" mode. Click the "Apply" button on any job listing. Fill in your details and upload your CV (PDF, DOC, or image). Wait for the CV to upload completely before submitting. Once submitted, you can track your application status in "My Activity".
How do I post a job vacancy?
Go to your dashboard and switch to "Hire Staff" mode, or click "Add Job" from the sidebar. Fill in all job details. If you are a regular User, your post goes to "Pending" status and needs admin approval. Prime Members, Admins and Owners can post jobs that go live immediately.
What is Prime Membership and what are its benefits?
Prime Membership (₹499/month) gives you access to exclusive job listings, the ability to post jobs that go live immediately (no approval needed), view candidate resumes, connect with employers directly, and get a verified Prime tag on your profile. Your tag is numbered (Prime, Prime2, Prime3...) based on how many times you've renewed.
How do I purchase Prime Membership?
Go to "Membership" from your dashboard sidebar. Scan the UPI QR code or use the UPI ID provided to pay ₹499. After payment, enter your UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) number in the provided field and click "Submit Request". Your membership will be activated by the admin within 24 hours after UTR verification.
Where do I find my UTR number?
After making a UPI payment, open your UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, etc.) and go to your transaction history. Open the payment you made to us and look for "UTR", "Transaction ID" or "Reference Number" – it's usually a 12-digit number. Copy this and paste it in the UTR field on our membership page.
What is the Prime tag numbering system?
Your Prime tag reflects how many times you've purchased membership. Your first membership gives you the "Prime" tag. The second renewal gives "Prime2", third gives "Prime3", and so on. Higher numbered Prime tags signal to employers that you are a long-term, committed platform member.
I forgot my Admin MPIN. What should I do?
Admin MPINs are set and managed by the Owner. If you've forgotten your MPIN, contact the Owner of the system. The Owner can set a new MPIN for you from the Owner Dashboard → Settings → Manage MPINs → Set Admin MPIN.
How do notifications work?
You receive in-app notifications when your requests (membership, contact, resume download) are approved or rejected. You can also receive broadcast notifications from the Owner. Look for the bell icon in your dashboard navbar to see your notifications. Allow browser notifications for instant alerts.
What can the HC AI Assistant do?
Our AI Assistant is powered by Google Gemini and can answer almost any question you have — about jobs, careers, the hospitality industry, interview tips, salary ranges, how to use the platform, payment process, and more. It speaks English, Hindi and Bangla. Just click the floating bot icon at the bottom-right of any page to start chatting.
How do I change the assistant's language?
Open the chat window and tap the language chips (EN / HI / BN) at the bottom of the chat. You can also just type your question in any of the three languages — the AI will reply in the same language automatically. The website language and assistant language are independent, so you can browse in English while chatting in Bangla.
How does the Hospitality Careers Resume Studio work?
Visit the Resume Studio page. Fill in 4 quick steps — your name, job target, experience, education, and skills. Click Generate My Resume and the Hospitality Careers system will craft a polished, ATS-friendly hospitality resume with a professional summary and quantified bullet points. You can preview it instantly and tap Download PDF to save it on your device.
Can I add my photo to the resume?
Yes! On Step 1 (Personal Details), you'll see a Profile Photo upload field at the top. Click Choose File, select any JPG or PNG (under 2 MB) from your phone or computer, and the photo will upload instantly with a live preview. The image is auto-resized for crisp printing and embedded directly into your downloadable PDF — no separate upload step, no waiting. To change or remove it, use the Remove photo button and pick a new one.
Do I need to fill every field?
No. Only Full Name and Job Title are required. Everything else — including the photo — is optional. Our system will infer sensible professional defaults from your job title if a field is empty. The more details you provide, the more accurate and personalized your resume will be.
Can I regenerate or edit the resume?
Yes — after generation, click the refresh button next to Download PDF to regenerate a fresh version. To change content, edit any input field (or swap the photo) and generate again. Each generation produces a unique professional version.
How do I install the app on Android?
Open hospitalitycareers in Chrome on your Android phone. After a few seconds you'll see an "Install Hospitality Careers" banner — tap Install. If the banner doesn't appear, open Chrome's menu (3 dots, top-right) and tap Install app or Add to Home screen. The app icon will appear on your home screen and launches like a normal app, full-screen, no browser bar.
How do I install the app on iPhone / iPad?
Open the site in Safari on your iPhone or iPad. Tap the Share button (square with up-arrow) at the bottom of the screen. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add. The Hospitality Careers icon will appear on your home screen — tap it to launch the app full-screen.
Does the app work offline?
Yes — basic shell, logo and styles are cached for offline access. Live data (jobs, applications, payments) needs an internet connection. If you're offline, you'll see a friendly "You're offline" screen until your connection returns.
How do I update the app?
Updates are automatic. Whenever we publish a new version, your installed app picks it up the next time you open it (refresh once to see changes). No need to visit any store.
What is HC Wallet?
HC Wallet is your personal in-app balance on Hospitality Careers. 1 point = ₹1. You can use the balance to pay for Prime Membership instantly without going through UPI again. Your wallet appears on your User / Prime dashboard.
How do I add money to my HC Wallet?
On your dashboard, click "Add Money" in the HC Wallet card. Enter the amount, click "Open UPI App" and pay to our UPI ID. After payment, paste the UTR / Transaction ID and click "Submit for Approval". Owner will verify and credit your wallet (usually within 24 hours). You'll get a notification when approved.
How do I pay for Prime using HC Wallet?
On the Membership page, you'll see a "Pay from HC Wallet" option below the regular UPI / QR options. If your wallet balance is enough, click it — your account will be upgraded to Prime instantly, no UTR or waiting needed. The amount is deducted from your wallet and shown in your transaction history.
Where can I see my wallet transactions?
On your dashboard, in the HC Wallet card, click the "History" button. You'll see every credit (top-up, referral bonus) and debit (Prime payment, withdrawal) with the date and note.
How do I withdraw my HC Wallet balance to UPI?
On your dashboard, click "Withdraw" in the HC Wallet card. Enter the amount (minimum ₹200), your UPI ID, and account holder name. Submit. Your wallet is debited immediately and the owner pays you via UPI within 24 hours. You'll get a notification once paid. If your request is rejected for any reason, the points are auto-refunded back to your wallet.
What are HC Coupons?
Coupons are special rewards sent by the owner. There are three types:
  • Discount Coupon — gives ₹X off your next Prime Membership purchase.
  • Free Trial Coupon — gives free Prime Membership for X days.
  • 1 Month Free Coupon — gives a full free month of Prime Membership.
How do I know I received a coupon?
You'll receive a notification the moment a coupon is sent to you. Open your dashboard — you'll see a new "My Coupons" card with the coupon details and a Claim button.
How do I claim and use a coupon?
Click "Claim" on the coupon.
• Discount coupons are saved to your account and auto-applied the next time you open the Membership page — you'll see the discounted price ready to pay.
• Trial / Free Month coupons activate Prime Membership instantly for the given days, no payment needed.
Do coupons expire?
Yes, every coupon shows its expiry date on the card. After expiry, the coupon is auto-disabled and cannot be claimed. Claim early to avoid losing it.
How does Refer & Earn work?
Share your unique referral link from the Refer & Earn card on your dashboard. When someone signs up using your link and becomes a Prime member, both of you automatically get 10 HC Wallet points (₹10). The reward is credited the moment the referred user's Prime payment is approved.
How do I share my referral link?
Open your dashboard → Refer & Earn card → click the link to copy, or use Share to send through WhatsApp, SMS, email, or any social app. Anyone who signs up using that link is permanently linked to your referral.
Where can I see how many people I referred?
On your dashboard, the Refer & Earn card shows Total Referred (everyone who signed up via your link) and Prime Conversions (those who became paying Prime members and triggered your bonus).
When do I get my referral reward?
The 10-point reward is credited automatically as soon as your referred user's Prime payment is approved by the owner — no claim or request needed. You'll get a notification, and the points will reflect in your wallet immediately.
Where can I see my referral history & status?
Open your dashboard → Refer & Earn card → click "My Referrals". You'll see every user who joined using your link with auto-detected status:
• 🆕 Just signed up — they joined but haven't completed profile
• 👤 Signed up · Profile complete — they're active but haven't bought Prime yet
• 👑 Prime member — converted, and you've already received 10 HC points
Status updates automatically — no enquiry needed.
How do I see status of my top-up / withdrawal?
Click History in the HC Wallet card. Every entry shows a colored status badge:
• ⏳ Pending — owner is reviewing
• ✓ Approved / Paid — done, points credited or money sent to your UPI
• ✗ Rejected — for withdrawals, points are auto-refunded back to your wallet
Pending items show an Enquiry button if you want to ask the owner about status.
What is the "Enquiry" button on pending items?
If your top-up, withdrawal, or coupon redemption is taking too long, click the Enquiry button on that pending row, write your question, and submit. It goes straight to the owner's Enquiries dashboard. You'll receive a notification when it's resolved. Note: Referrals don't need enquiry — their status updates automatically.
Can I delete a resume from "My Resumes"?
Yes! In your dashboard's My Resumes section, every saved resume has two buttons: 📄 Download (PDF) and 🗑️ Delete. Click delete and confirm — the resume is permanently removed from your account. You can always create a new one with the AI Resume Builder.
Can I see my password while typing?
Yes — both the Login form and the Sign Up form now show a small 👁️ eye icon inside the password field. Click it once to show your password, click again to hide it. Helpful when typing on mobile to avoid mistakes.
Why do I have to select a State during profile completion?
Your State helps us show you jobs nearest to you and lets the right local Admin / Branch Head help you faster. Pick any state from the dropdown — you can update it later from your profile.
Is the referral reward always 10 HC points?
10 HC points is the default, but the owner can change it anytime from the dashboard. Whatever value is currently set will be shown live on your Refer & Earn card — both you and your friend always get the same number of points when they buy Prime.
Can referrers get special coupons too?
Yes! Owner can target a coupon to "🎁 Referrers" — meaning everyone who has referred at least one Prime member. They'll get the coupon as a notification automatically; click the notification → My Coupons → Claim.
What can an Admin / Branch Head do?
Admins are promoted by the Owner with a specific role (Branch Head, Team Leader, Accountant, Recruiter, etc.) and assigned to one or more states. The Owner gives them only the permissions they need — like approving job posts, wallet top-ups, withdrawals, sending coupons, handling enquiries, etc. So your local admin only sees & manages users / jobs from their assigned state.
