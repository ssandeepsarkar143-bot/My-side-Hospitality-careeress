Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 April 2026

Hospitality Careers (operated under the brand Hospitality Careers by the Hospitality Careers management team) is a hospitality job portal built for verified candidates and employers across India. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and the choices you have. By using findhospitalitycareers.web.app you agree to this policy.

🔒 Security commitment

We never ask for your password, MPIN, OTP, UPI PIN, debit/credit card CVV, or any banking PIN over phone, SMS, email or chat. If anyone claims to be from Hospitality Careers and asks for these, please ignore them and report to hospitalitycareers01@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — your name, email, phone number, profile photo, and password (stored only as a one-way hash via Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it).
  • Profile data — work experience, skills, education, languages, certifications, hospitality department, preferred locations, that you choose to add to your resume.
  • Job activity — jobs you view, save, apply to, or request employer contact for.
  • Payment metadata — UPI transaction reference (UTR) you submit for Prime membership. We do not store your UPI PIN, full card number, CVV, or banking credentials.
  • Communication — feedback, support tickets and messages you send us.
  • Device & log data — browser type, IP address, anonymised analytics events (page visits, button clicks) collected via Google Analytics 4 to improve the product.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To match you with relevant hospitality job listings and notify you of new jobs.
  • To process Prime membership requests after you submit a UPI payment.
  • To respond to feedback, support requests and contact-employer requests.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • To comply with applicable Indian law.

3. How we store and protect your data

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore + Firebase Storage), which is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication, an industry-standard identity service used by major brands worldwide. We apply role-based Firestore security rules so that only you (and authorised reviewers) can access your records.

4. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only with:

  • Verified employers — only after you explicitly request employer contact for a specific job.
  • Service providers — Google Firebase (hosting, database, auth), Google Analytics (anonymised analytics), Cloudflare (chatbot AI delivery). These providers process data under their own strict policies.
  • Legal authorities — when required by valid legal process.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies (and similar local-storage keys) for session management, language preference and Google Analytics 4. You can clear them anytime from your browser settings. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

6. Your rights

  • Access & correction — log in and edit your profile anytime.
  • Deletion — email hospitalitycareers01@gmail.com from your registered email and we will delete your account and personal data within 7 working days, except records we must keep for legal/financial reasons.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the platform anytime; close your account by emailing us.

7. Children

Hospitality Careers is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent version. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice.
